Website summary:

What will this content contain?

This content will contain Custom made Kpop decks, buy/trade/sell as well as forums for people to collaborate or talk about their favorite groups.

Where will you get all of the content for your site (ie. images & text)?

Pictures can be used that the users took. For what is going to be sold in a pack or set, that will be the photocards owned, with a white or back blackdrop.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Users can connect with other fans, talk about their bias's and get their own custom decks, or if they are looking for a single card to complete their set, or they have specific people they want, they can pick and choose. This will save them money on buying multiple decks.

Why should a site be built for this?

I want there to be more options for the community rather than a few etsy sellers. I want them to feel like they have enough to choose from.

What would you like users to do at your site?

Collaborate, engage, explore, talk, and have fun looking and picking out cards.

What is the goal of the site?

The goal of this site is to provide a space for the kpop community to connect.
