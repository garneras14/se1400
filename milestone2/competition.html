Competition

What did the competition do right?

I loved how both of them have easy navigation. They both are organized and easy to use. The navigation is smooth and theres unique things about both sites. One has live videos you can watch, Etsy has drop down buttons, to make finding the categories easier.

What did the competition do wrong?

On Etsy in particular, I would put the catagories button aside underneath the Etsy button and move the search bar so its more in the center. For Ebay, the slide bar towrads the bottom, by selling. I would make those into more of a chart than a slide, that way the user doesnt need to click or navigate to look at something that could be on one section. I would probably also take away the cart and notification popups when you hover over it.
